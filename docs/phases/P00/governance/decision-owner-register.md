## Notes

1. **Coverage.** The rows hold the 52-ID required set of phase-plan §7.5 exactly once: D-01…D-20 as Primary IDs with their mirror
   ADRs (ADR-013…ADR-032, plus ADR-011 for D-12 and ADR-012 for D-20) in Linked IDs, and ADR-002, ADR-003, ADR-004, ADR-006,
   ADR-007…ADR-010, ADR-033 and ADR-034 as their own rows. The set equals the snapshot in `decisions.md` §2.
2. **Needed by** is the earliest phase whose plan, tests or implementation depend on the answer. The rules used: a decision that
   P01 confirms by its DoD or that changes the P01 domain model, auth architecture or threat model is needed by P01; a decision
   that adds or removes a schema field, endpoint, media type or error semantics is needed by P02; template and design details are
   needed by P10; library defaults by P07.
3. **Deadline.** P01 is the ADR phase. Decisions needed by P02 or later that P01 can prepare get `before P01 gate`, so the P02
   contract starts from accepted decisions. Decisions needed by P01 itself get `before P01 implementation`, which means the ADR
   draft is part of the P01 architecture plan and is accepted with it. Template decisions get `before P10 implementation`. Every
   deadline is at or before its phase-plan §7.5 anchor; the Notes give the anchor as "Upper bound" where it is later than the
   proposal. These are proposals: if the owner wants a smaller P01 batch, any row can move to a later deadline up to its Needed
   by phase (Q-1, K-12/SC-12).
4. **Preparer** is the role that drafts the ADR in the deadline phase. The Contract Architect prepares only ADR-006, which is a
   contract-format decision.
5. **Decider** is `Product owner` for every row (PD-P00-04). No delegation is recorded; owner question Q-3 asks whether technical
   ADRs (for example ADR-002) are delegated.
6. **Release blocker** is `yes` only where a source calls the item a release blocker or release gate: ADR-012 ("release
   blocker") and ARCHITECTURE.md §6 ("EU residency are release gates"), both on the D-20 row. `no` does not mean the item may stay
   open: every item must be decided by its deadline (DECISION-REGISTER.md Gate policy).
7. **Excluded-pending items** D-07…D-10 have deadlines before the P02 contract, so the contract guard of GOV-03 is checked
   against an answer rather than against the interim reading of PD-P00-05.
8. **Unregistered candidates** have no register ID. P00 creates none; the owner is asked to let P01 assign IDs (phase-plan §14
   OOS-02). Their Preparer and Deadline follow the same rules as the register rows.
9. **Citations** use the strict format of phase-plan §7.1 item 13 (owner-approved default I-01). Documents without numbered
   headings (`Pxx/PHASE.md`, `TEST-STRATEGY.md`, `TRACEABILITY.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `reference/README.md`) are cited as
   `<doc> header`; the cell text names the heading meant (Objective, DoD, Gate policy).
